05 / About

Curiosity below the surface.

I'm a postdoctoral research associate in Environmental Sciences at the University of Virginia, working at the intersection of permafrost science, geophysics, hydrology, and environmental change.

My path began in environmental geology and laboratory investigations of gas hydrates and frozen sediments. Today, I connect that subsurface perspective to field-scale measurements of Arctic landscapes and the places people build on them.

My aim is to explain not only how frozen ground responds to changing climate, but why those responses are so uneven across nearby sites — and how better understanding can inform Arctic resilience.
