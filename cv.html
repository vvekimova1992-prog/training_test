Research experience

University of Virginia 2024–present

  • Study how snow, vegetation, water drainage, infrastructure, and subsurface conditions contribute to heterogeneous Arctic permafrost thaw.
  • Integrate micrometeorological data from five Arctic sites over four seasons.
  • Analyze electrical resistivity tomography and ground-penetrating radar surveys to characterize near-surface ground conditions.
  • Develop snow-cover metrics from Sentinel-2 imagery and investigate Arctic water quality.

Institute of Geosphere Dynamics, Russian Academy of Sciences 2022–2024

Junior Research Scientist · Experimental research on salt migration, ice melting, and sediment properties in frozen ground.

Skoltech, Center for Hydrocarbon Recovery 2017–2024

Research Engineer / Research Intern · Experimental studies of gas hydrate stability, dissociation, methane recovery, and coupled heat and mass transfer.

Lomonosov Moscow State University 2012–2016

Engineer · Frozen sediment and gas hydrate stability experiments.
